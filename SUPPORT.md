## Getting help

- **Questions?** Open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/albertsdk/hsync/discussions) in
  the main repository.
- **Found a bug?** Open an issue in the relevant project repository.
- **Security vulnerability?** Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting —
  see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open public issues for
  security-sensitive matters.

## 💚 Solo project

HSYNC is a passion project maintained in spare time by volunteers. Feature requests, bug reports, and pull requests are reviewed as soon as possible, but response times may vary. Thank you for your patience and support!
